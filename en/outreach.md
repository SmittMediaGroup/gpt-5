# First-touch emails to agencies (EN)

> Draft, 2026-09-25. Each email carries **one** fact in the format `[fact: query, AI system, date, who was named]`. Before sending, replace it with a real observation from my own run on the agency's client or niche. Never send a made-up fact. Each body is 5 lines, not counting the greeting and signature.
>
> Who to send to: agencies and consultancies outside Russia that sell SEO or market entry for Russian-speaking audiences (CIS, Russian-market entry, brand relocation, Kazakhstan / Kyrgyzstan / Uzbekistan). Before writing, check that the recipient isn't a sanctioned person or entity.

## Notes for use

- **One fact, verifiable.** Keep the screenshot and the raw answer with date and time. If the agency asks, send both.
- **Don't overclaim.** A single answer is an observation, not a share. Don't write "Alice never names you" based on one run. Write "on [date], for [query], Alice named X and Y".
- **Follow-up:** one reminder after 4–5 business days, with a second, different fact. Then stop.
- **Payment and compliance** are discussed only after interest is confirmed: invoice via [placeholder: payment method], plus the standard compliance note (I don't serve sanctioned entities; the buyer checks their own obligations).
- **Opt-out line** (add if the local law of the recipient's country requires it): "If this isn't relevant, reply 'no' and I won't write again."
