## Notes

**Top 5 channels to start with:**
1. **Direct outreach to agencies that already sell Yandex / Russian SEO** (#1). Use the 3 email variants in `outreach.md`, one real fact per email. 10–20 emails a week is realistic for a solo studio.
2. **CIS market-entry and CIS advertising agencies as resellers** (#2–#5). Offer white-label and a partner price. They carry the client relationship and the compliance checks on their side.
3. **Grow Your Agency Slack, #llm-visibility** (#7). A free, agency-owner audience. Post data (Alice vs ChatGPT gaps with intervals), not ads.
4. **A Search Engine Land guest pitch with original data** (#11). One published data piece becomes the proof for every other channel: Reddit, SEOFOMO, directories.
5. **Free directory profiles: TechBehemoths plus Clutch, GoodFirms and DesignRush** (#20, #14–#16). A few hours once, then passive credibility. Don't pay for sponsorship before the first 3 reviews.

**What the table says about platforms.** The big Western freelance marketplaces (Upwork, Fiverr, Contra) are closed to Russia-based sellers. Freelancer.com is restricted, and PeoplePerHour and Toptal are unclear. So the sales motion is **B2B direct + partners + content**, not marketplace bidding. The profile text in `profile.md` works just as well as a directory profile or a partner one-pager.

**Why Kazakhstan / Kyrgyzstan / Uzbekistan need a different angle.** In Kazakhstan Google leads search (StatCounter: Google 73.64%, Yandex 24.78–28.31% in 2026; snippet, https://gs.statcounter.com/search-engine-market-share/all/kazakhstan). There, pitch the audit as "Russian-language AI answers across Alice *and* ChatGPT / Perplexity", not "Yandex first". In Russia (Yandex ≈70–73%, StatCounter via snippets; https://gs.statcounter.com/search-engine-market-share/all/russian-federation), Alice comes first. Kyrgyzstan and Uzbekistan: no data on search share (not searched).
